# Figure 2 — Caption

## English caption

**Figure 2.** Median principal fault displacement profiles versus normalized
along-rupture position *x/L*, organized by faulting style. Top two rows show
the normalized profiles *D/MD* (a–c) and *D/AD* (d–f); the bottom row shows
absolute median displacement at *m* = 6.5 on a log axis (g–i). Columns are
normal (a, d, g), reverse (b, e, h), and strike-slip (c, f, i). Profiles are
symmetric about *x/L* = 0.5, so only the half-profile is shown, and each
curve is plotted only within its published applicable magnitude and *x/L*
range (Valentini et al., 2025, Table 4). For Petersen et al. (2011) in panel
(f) the source-defined *D/AD* lognormal coefficients (Eqs. 14–17) are used;
the model is absent from panel (c) because its source publication does not
define a *D/MD* distribution. For Moss and Ross (2011) the Gamma and Weibull
*D/AD* variants (Eqs. 6–7) are both plotted in panel (e) and their Beta
*D/MD* variant in panel (b). Moss et al. (2024) appears only in the normalized
panels (b, e) because its source publishes only normalized *D/MD* / *D/AD*
distributions, following Valentini et al. (2025) Figure 14.
