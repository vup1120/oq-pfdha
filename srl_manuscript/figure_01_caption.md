# Figure 1 — Caption

## English caption

**Figure 1.** Conditional probability of primary (principal) surface rupture
versus moment magnitude *m* for the implemented prediction models, organized by
faulting style: (A) normal, (B) reverse, (C) strike-slip, and (D)
style-independent ("all-slip"). Each curve is plotted only within its published
applicable magnitude range (Valentini et al., 2025, Table 4). Mammarella et al.
(2024) curves use a seismogenic thickness of 9 km, dip angles of 50°, 30°, and
90° for normal, reverse, and strike-slip respectively, and three magnitude
scaling relations (MSR 0–2).
